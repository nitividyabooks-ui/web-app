## PRODUCT DESCRIPTION
Give your child the gift of values that last a lifetime.

Nititales Stories That Shape Values is a collection of five beautifully illustrated moral stories by Monika Sharma, crafted to spark big conversations between parents and little ones aged 2–5 years. From courage on a wobbly bike to the magic of saying "please" and "thank you," every page plants a seed of character.

This single hardcover brings five complete stories to life: Miko’s Park Day Adventure, where young Miko learns to never give up after a tumble on his bike; Ready, Set, Oops!, where siblings Tino and Tini own up to a mistake and learn that honesty and responsibility go hand in hand; Minie’s Big Heroes, where tiny goldfish Minie discovers that her small size is her greatest strength; Broody the Kind Bear, a story of empathy and the joy of sharing; and The Magic Button: A Rug Road Adventure, where good manners and teamwork save the day.

Each story teaches a core life value — resilience, kindness, empathy, responsibility, and good manners — through relatable characters and sensory language that keeps children engaged from the first page to the last. These are not just bedtime stories; they are conversations waiting to happen, helping children aged 2–5 build emotional intelligence naturally.

Unlike books that preach, Nititales weaves values into exciting adventures featuring lovable characters: Miko the elephant, sibling duo Tino and Tini, little goldfish Minie, gentle Broody the Bear, and the racing vehicles Reddie and Greenie. Rhythmic language, onomatopoeia like "ZOOM! ZIP!", and vibrant full-colour illustrations make every read-aloud a joyful bonding experience.

Printed as a sturdy hardcover with 80 full-colour pages, the book measures 8 x 8 inches — perfectly sized for little hands. Thick, high-quality pages are built to survive the enthusiasm of toddlers.

An outstanding choice for children aged 2–5 as a bedtime story book, classroom read-aloud, or thoughtful gift for birthdays, Diwali, Children’s Day (November 14), or school prizes. A book that parents, grandparents, and teachers will reach for again and again.

Order Nititales Stories That Shape Values today — stories that do more than entertain, stories that shape who your child becomes.
