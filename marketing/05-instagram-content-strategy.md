# Instagram Content Strategy

> Goal: Rebuild organic reach, grow from 200 to 1,000 followers in 60 days,
> and turn Instagram into a consistent traffic source for Amazon and website.

---

## Why Views Dropped (And How to Fix It)

When you started "promoting" the books, you likely:
- Posted content that looked like ads (product photos with buy links)
- Reduced the ratio of genuinely useful/entertaining content
- Got fewer comments/saves (people scroll past ads, they don't engage)
- Instagram detected low engagement → stopped pushing to non-followers

**The fix is simple but requires discipline: stop selling on Instagram. Start giving.**

Sell happens at the end of a journey. Instagram is the beginning of the journey.

## Content Pillars (The 5 Types of Posts You'll Rotate)

### Pillar 1: Indian Culture Education (40% of content)
Teach parents something about Indian culture they can share with their child. NO mention of the books. Pure value.

**Examples:**
- "What does Diwali mean? Here's how to explain it to a 3-year-old in 30 seconds" (Reel)
- "5 Hindi words your toddler will love saying" (Reel with text)
- "The story of Ganesha — told in 60 seconds for tiny humans" (Reel)
- "Why we touch elders' feet — the meaning behind a beautiful tradition"
- "Indian lullabies that every parent should know" (audio + text)

**Why this works:** Parents will save and share this. Saves and shares are the highest-value signals to Instagram. This builds your authority as the Indian cultural parenting account.

### Pillar 2: Parenting Moments (25% of content)
Relatable Indian parenting content — no books, just connection with your audience.

**Examples:**
- "The chaos of reading to a toddler at bedtime (told honestly)" (funny reel)
- "When your toddler only wants to read the SAME book 47 times in a row 😅"
- "Things my Indian parents said vs. what I say to my kids" (trending format)
- "Screen time vs books: what actually worked for us"
- "POV: explaining Holi to a 2-year-old" (funny)

**Why this works:** Relatability builds followers. People follow accounts that feel like they understand their life.

### Pillar 3: Miko Read-Aloud (20% of content)
Short clips of the actual books being read to a real child.

**Examples:**
- Film someone reading 2–3 pages from "Miko Celebrates Festivals" to their toddler
- Show the child's face and reaction — this is gold
- Add captions on screen for the bilingual text
- "Read along with us" format with sound

**Why this works:** This is the most direct, authentic sales content. A parent watching their child's equivalent react with delight is better than any ad copy. This IS the ad — but it doesn't feel like one.

**Casting the read-alouds:**
- Ask friends/family with real toddlers to be in the video
- Authentic homes, not studios — messy backgrounds are fine
- Child's reaction is everything — the book is secondary

### Pillar 4: Behind the Scenes (10% of content)
Show the brand's humanity.

**Examples:**
- "How we decided what Miko should look like" — design sketches to final character
- "Why we made Miko a bilingual book" — founder story
- "First time we saw our books in a box" — unboxing moment
- "How a children's book goes from idea to print" — the process

**Why this works:** People buy from brands they know and like. BTS content builds the know-like-trust factor.

### Pillar 5: Community (5% of content)
Once you have customers, turn them into content.

**Examples:**
- Repost parents' photos of their children with Miko books
- "Miko Reader of the Week" — photo of a child + their parent's quote
- "Tell us your child's favourite Miko book" — story poll → post the results

## Reel Format That Gets Views

### The 3-Second Rule
The first 3 seconds determine if someone watches. You need a hook.

**Text Hook Formulas (display on screen for first 2 seconds):**
- "The Hindi word no one teaches their toddler anymore 🇮🇳"
- "What to say when your child asks 'Who is Ganesh?'"
- "Indian parents will relate to this 😅"
- "I read to my toddler every night for 30 days — here's what changed"
- "The Diwali activity toddlers obsess over"

### Audio Strategy
- Use trending audio (search by "most used" in Reels audio tab)
- Original audio (you speaking) works well for educational content
- Hindi music that's currently trending → instant cultural signal

### Video Length
- 7–15 seconds: Best for algorithm push to non-followers
- 20–30 seconds: Good for educational content that needs time
- 45–60 seconds: Only for high-value content (story, BTS) — must be engaging throughout

### Captions
- First line must continue the hook: "Here's what I told my daughter about Diwali..."
- Add 3–5 relevant hashtags in the caption (not 30)
- Use hashtags: #IndianParenting #ToddlerBooks #BilingualKids #IndianMom #MikoBooks #NitiVidya #DesiParenting #HindiLearning

## Growth Tactics

### Collaboration Posts
The single fastest way to grow. Find ONE account with 10K–50K followers in Indian parenting space and do a collab post:
- You create the content together (or separately with shared concept)
- Both accounts post it as a collab — it appears in BOTH feeds
- Their 30K followers see your account for the first time
- Target: 1 collab per month minimum

**How to pitch a collab:**
"Hi [name], I love your content about [specific thing]. We run @nitividya — Indian cultural books for toddlers. Would you be open to a collab post? I was thinking [specific idea] — could be great for both our audiences. Happy to send you a copy of our Miko books as a thank you!"

### Hashtag Strategy (Realistic)
Don't use mega hashtags like #parenting (300M posts — you'll never rank).
Use medium hashtags (100K–500K posts) where you can actually appear:
- #IndianParenting (use)
- #DesiParenting (use)
- #BilingualKids (use)
- #ToddlerBooksIndia (use)
- #IndianMomsOfInstagram (use)
- #NRIParenting (use)
- #HindiLearning (use)

### Engage Before You Post
30 minutes before posting a Reel: go and genuinely comment on 10–15 posts in your hashtags. This signals activity to the algorithm and your comments get you seen by potential followers.
