# Go-To-Market Strategy — Phase by Phase

> Three phases, each with a clear goal, clear actions, and clear success metric.
> Do NOT skip a phase. Each phase builds the foundation for the next.

---

## Why Phases Matter

Running ads without reviews = burning money.
Running influencer campaigns without a WhatsApp/email list = losing every lead.
Building a website audience without a product worth talking about = confusion.

Each phase solves the blockers that stop the next phase from working.

---

## Phase 1: Foundation (Now — Week 8)

**Goal: Get the first 20 sales and 10 reviews. Build a list of 200 warm leads.**

You are here. Nothing works until this is done.

### What You're Building
- Social proof (reviews on Amazon)
- Trust signals (real photos of real children with the books)
- A way to capture leads (lead magnet on website)
- A WhatsApp audience to market to
- Content engine on Instagram

### Actions (In Priority Order)

#### Week 1–2: Reviews Sprint
- [ ] Ask 10–15 friends/family members to buy from Amazon and leave a review
  - Give them the Amazon link, ask them to buy (not receive as gift) for a verified review
  - Do this privately — Amazon cannot know you're incentivising reviews
  - Reimburse outside the platform if needed
- [ ] Enable "Request a Review" on every Amazon order (Seller Central → Orders)
- [ ] If Brand Registered on Amazon: Enroll bundle in **Amazon Vine** for 15–20 editorial reviews

#### Week 1–2: Lead Magnet
- [ ] Create a **Free Activity Kit** (PDF): "10 Fun Learning Activities Inspired by the Miko Books"
  - Design in Canva (2–3 hours)
  - Include: colouring pages of Miko, Hindi-English word matching, festival dot-to-dot, animal sounds in Hindi
  - Upload to website with an email gate
- [ ] Add email capture form above the fold on homepage: "Download Free Miko Activity Kit →"

#### Week 1–2: WhatsApp Setup
- [ ] Create WhatsApp broadcast list
- [ ] Add click-to-chat button on Instagram bio with message: "Hi! I'd like to join the Miko Parents Club"
- [ ] Set up automated welcome message with free activity kit link
- [ ] Add QR code inside all books (on next print run): "Join our WhatsApp"

#### Week 1–4: Instagram Reset
- [ ] Stop all promotional/sales content immediately
- [ ] Shift to 5 content pillars (see `05-instagram-content-strategy.md`)
- [ ] Post 5–6 Reels per week minimum
- [ ] First collab: DM 20 Indian parenting accounts for a collab post

#### Week 2–4: Micro-Influencer Outreach
- [ ] Identify 50 Indian parenting Instagram accounts (5K–100K followers)
- [ ] DM them with free book offer (see `07-influencer-playbook.md`)
- [ ] Goal: 5 influencers posting about Miko in first month
- [ ] Ask each influencer for: Instagram post + Amazon link in bio

#### Week 4–8: Community
- [ ] Join 10 Indian parenting Facebook groups
- [ ] Post genuine parenting content 2–3x/week (no spam, no links)
- [ ] After establishing presence, share the free activity kit link
- [ ] Join parenting WhatsApp groups (ask friends to add you as admin or member)

### Phase 1 Success Criteria
- ✅ 10+ Amazon reviews on the bundle
- ✅ 200+ people on WhatsApp list
- ✅ 200+ email list
- ✅ 5+ influencer posts with Amazon link
- ✅ Instagram reaching 1,000 followers
- ✅ 25+ total sales

### Do NOT Start in Phase 1
- ❌ Meta ads (no social proof, no pixel data)
- ❌ Amazon PPC scaling (no reviews = no conversion)
- ❌ New product development (focus on selling what you have)

## Phase 2: Momentum (Week 8 — Week 20)

**Goal: First 100 sales. Build owned audience of 1,000+ leads. Establish Instagram presence.**

### What You're Building
- Repeatable sales system (Amazon + website)
- Retargeting audiences (Facebook Pixel data)
- Customer community (Instagram + WhatsApp)
- Amazon organic ranking (BSR improvement from reviews + consistent sales)

### Actions

#### Amazon PPC (Now Ready Because You Have Reviews)
- Activate the PPC strategy from `04-amazon-strategy.md`
- Start at ₹200/day budget, scale only when ACOS < 60%
- Focus keywords: mythology books, bilingual books, indian culture books for kids
- Add Sponsored Products Auto Campaign to discover terms

#### Meta Ads — Start Small
- Install Facebook Pixel properly (track: page view, add to cart, purchase, email signup)
- Create a **website traffic campaign**: ₹300/day targeting Indian parents 25–40
- CTA: "Download Free Miko Activity Kit" → captures email
- Retarget website visitors with product ads (for anyone who visited but didn't buy)

#### Instagram — Scale Content
- You should be at 1,000–2,000 followers by now
- Start Instagram story polls to involve your audience
- Introduce "Miko Monday" — read-aloud Reel every Monday featuring a page from the books
- Post customer photos (UGC) as Reels — "Look at Aryan with his Miko books!"

#### WhatsApp Nurture Sequence
- You should have 200–500 leads by now
- Send a 4-message nurture sequence (see `06-lead-gen-whatsapp-email.md`)
- Announce new book launches to WhatsApp FIRST before anywhere else

#### Website
- Add customer reviews/photos section
- Add blog: 2 posts/week (Indian parenting tips, cultural education, Miko activities)
- Start email newsletter: "The Miko Letter" — monthly parenting + culture content

### Phase 2 Success Criteria
- ✅ 100+ total sales
- ✅ 50+ Amazon reviews
- ✅ 1,000+ Instagram followers
- ✅ 500+ WhatsApp subscribers
- ✅ 500+ email subscribers
- ✅ Website converting at 1%+
- ✅ ACOS on Amazon PPC < 70%

## Phase 3: Scale (Month 5 Onwards)

**Goal: Establish NitiVidya as the brand Indian parents recommend to each other.**

### What You're Building
- Brand recognition in Indian parenting communities
- New book titles driving customers back
- Ambassador/affiliate program (parents selling to other parents)
- Potentially: offline channels (bookstores, school libraries, gift shops)

### Actions

#### New Miko Book Launch
- Tease upcoming book on WhatsApp and email BEFORE it launches
- Pre-order campaign on website (build waitlist)
- Launch on Amazon with existing review base behind you
- Influencer campaign amplifying launch

#### Parent Ambassador Program
- Your happiest customers become your salespeople
- "Earn ₹100 for every friend who buys" — affiliate referral link
- Give superfans a "Miko Ambassador" badge on Instagram
- These parents post organically — zero ad spend

#### Gifting Partnerships
- Partner with baby/parenting gift box services (GiftTree India, etc.)
- Include Miko books in curated gift boxes for baby showers
- Target corporate gifting for baby announcements

#### Offline Presence
- School library donations (10 books to 5 schools → photos → social content + brand credibility)
- Playgroup distribution in urban metros
- Indian grocery stores and gift shops in the NRI market (UK/US)

### Phase 3 Success Criteria
- ✅ 500+ total sales
- ✅ 100+ Amazon reviews
- ✅ 5,000+ Instagram followers
- ✅ 2,000+ WhatsApp subscribers
- ✅ 2+ new book titles launched
- ✅ NitiVidya mentioned organically in Indian parenting groups
