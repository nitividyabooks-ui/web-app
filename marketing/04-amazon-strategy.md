# Amazon Strategy — Listings + PPC

## Should You Run Amazon Ads Right Now?

**Short answer: Pause or drastically reduce. Here's why.**

### The Conversion Math
Your Amazon PPC over 10 days:
- 286 impressions → 7 clicks → 0 sales
- CTR: 2.45% (acceptable)
- Conversion rate: **0%**

The problem isn't the ads. The problem is **what happens after the click**.

When a parent clicks your ad, they land on a listing with:
- 0 reviews
- No star rating
- No social proof at all

No matter how good your ad is, **Indian parents won't buy a children's book with zero reviews** when alternatives with 50–500 reviews exist.

Every click you pay for right now is ₹13 wasted.

### What to Do With the Current Campaign
**Option A (Recommended): Pause completely** until you have 10+ reviews.
- Daily budget: ₹0 for 3–4 weeks
- Let the review sprint happen (Phase 1 actions)
- Restart with confidence once reviews are live

**Option B: Keep on minimal spend** (₹50/day, not ₹350)
- Keep only 3–4 best-performing keywords to maintain a small presence
- Don't optimize aggressively — just stay visible

## Listing Optimization (Do This NOW, Free)

This is the most important thing you can do on Amazon today. Better listing = better organic rank + better conversion when ads do run.

### Title Optimization

**Current title (bundle):**
"NitiVidya Early Learning Collection – 5 Book Set for Kids 0-5 Years | Bilingual (Hindi & English) Picture Books on Indian Culture..."

**Problems:** Starts with brand name (no search volume), buries key terms

**Recommended:**
"Indian Cultural Picture Books for Kids 0-5 Years | 5 Book Miko Early Learning Set | Bilingual Hindi English | Festivals, Gods, Animals, Manners, Actions | NitiVidya"

"INDIAN CULTURE FROM DAY ONE: The Miko series introduces babies and toddlers to Indian festivals, Gods, values, and stories — the things no Western children's book covers. Finally, books that feel like your home."

"BILINGUAL HINDI & ENGLISH: Every page carries both languages — perfect for families who want to nurture Hindi language skills alongside English, or simply preserve the language of home."

**Bullet 4 — Age and format:**
"DESIGNED FOR 0–5 YEARS: A4 size for little hands, bold illustrations, simple text, 24 pages per book. Ideal for bedtime reading, learning time, or independent picture browsing."

"THE PERFECT INDIAN BABY GIFT: A thoughtful alternative to generic gift sets — this bundle is ideal for baby showers, naming ceremonies, 1st birthdays, Diwali, or any occasion where you want to give something meaningful."

### Images (Most Important After Reviews)

## Amazon Reviews — The Only Metric That Matters Right Now

| Reviews | Typical Amazon Conversion Rate |
|---------|-------------------------------|
| 0 | ~0.1–0.5% |
| 5–10 | ~1–2% |
| 25+ | ~3–5% |
| 100+ | ~5–8% |

Getting from 0 to 25 reviews = 10x conversion rate improvement.
No amount of ad spend or listing optimization comes close to this.

### Review Acquisition Plan
1. **Friends & Family Buy**: 10–15 people, real purchases, real reviews
2. **Amazon Vine**: If Brand Registered — enroll now (₹0 cost beyond free books)
3. **Request a Review**: Every order, every time, no exceptions
4. **Post-purchase insert card** (inside the book): "Loved Miko? Your review on Amazon helps other parents discover us." Include QR code to review page.
5. **WhatsApp/Email follow-up**: After confirmed delivery, send message: "Hope [child's name] loved the Miko books! If you have 2 minutes, an Amazon review makes a huge difference for our small publishing house 🙏"

## Amazon A+ Content (If Brand Registered)

If you are registered under Amazon Brand Registry, you can add A+ Content to your listing — this replaces the product description with rich visuals.

A+ Content increases conversion by 5–10% on average.

### A+ Content Layout for Bundle
1. **Hero image**: All 5 books open to their best pages
2. **Module 1**: "Why Miko?" — the problem we solve, beautiful illustration
3. **Module 2**: Book-by-book overview — cover + 3 bullet points per book
4. **Module 3**: "What parents say" (once you have reviews)
5. **Module 4**: "Perfect for..." gifting occasions image grid
6. **Module 5**: About NitiVidya — the brand story

This is worth 3–4 hours to build. Do it before scaling ads.
