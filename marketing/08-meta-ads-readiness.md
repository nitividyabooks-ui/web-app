# Meta Ads — When to Start, What to Build First

> Meta ads CAN be powerful for NitiVidya — but only after the right foundation exists.
> Starting too early burns budget with nothing to show for it.

---

## Why Meta Ads Aren't Ready Today

### Problem 1: No Social Proof
A parent clicks your Instagram ad → lands on Amazon (0 reviews) or your website (0 testimonials) → closes the tab.

The ad worked. The landing experience failed. You paid ₹15–30/click for nothing.

### Problem 2: No Pixel Data
Meta's algorithm is powerful, but it needs data to optimise. Without:
- Purchase events (people who bought)
- Add-to-cart events
- Email signups
- Sustained website traffic

...the algorithm has nothing to learn from and will spend your budget on the wrong people.

### Problem 3: No Lead Magnet = No Value Exchange
If you run a traffic campaign to your website today, why would a parent give their email or phone? There's no reason. Traffic without conversion is just paying for pageviews.

### Problem 4: Creative Hasn't Been Tested
You don't yet know what message or visual resonates. Instagram organic content is your free testing ground. The content that performs best organically = the ad creative that will work.

---

## What to Build Before Running Meta Ads

| Pre-Requisite | Status | Target |
|--------------|--------|--------|
| Facebook Pixel installed on website | Not confirmed | Done before Phase 2 |
| Pixel tracking purchase events | Not confirmed | Done before Phase 2 |
| 10+ Amazon reviews | 0 reviews | Phase 1 goal |
| Free activity kit lead magnet live | Not built | Build this week |
| Email capture form on website | Not confirmed | Build this week |
| 3+ customer photos/testimonials | 0 | Phase 1 goal |
| Instagram content that gets 2,000+ views | <500 views | Phase 1 goal |

When at least 5 of these 7 are checked, Meta ads are ready.

## Meta Ad Strategy (For When You're Ready)

### Campaign Type 1: Lead Generation Campaign
**Goal:** Capture emails for your list
**Budget:** ₹300–500/day to start
**Audience:** Indian parents, 25–40, interests = parenting, Indian culture, baby products, early learning
**Ad:** "Free Miko Activity Kit — 10 printable Indian learning activities for your toddler"
**CTA:** Download Now → website email capture page
**Expected:** ₹15–40 cost per email signup

### Campaign Type 2: Website Traffic + Retargeting
**Phase:** Run after getting pixel data from lead gen campaign
**Goal:** Retarget website visitors who didn't buy
**Audience A:** People who visited product page but didn't buy (Custom Audience: website visitors)
**Audience B:** People who downloaded activity kit but didn't buy (email list Custom Audience)
**Ad:** Social proof focused — customer photo + "X families love Miko"
**CTA:** Shop Now → Amazon bundle or website

### Campaign Type 3: NRI Market
**Phase:** Phase 2–3
**Goal:** Reach high-value Indian diaspora buyers (USA, UK, Canada, Australia)
**Audience:** Expats from India, Indian diaspora, interested in Indian culture
**Why higher value:** NRI parents pay full international price, have gifting culture, will buy for relatives in India too
**Budget:** ₹300/day targeting multiple countries

## Ad Creative That Will Work (When You're Ready)

### Ad Type 1: The Read-Aloud Video
**Format:** 15-second Reel-style video
**Content:** Real toddler being read a page from Miko, showing their reaction
**Hook text:** "Indian festivals, explained to a 2-year-old 🇮🇳"
**Why:** Authentic, emotional, shows the product in use

### Ad Type 2: The Problem/Solution Carousel
**Format:** 3-card carousel
- Card 1: "Most children's books show Western festivals, Western characters, Western stories."
- Card 2: "We made Miko — Indian festivals, Indian Gods, Indian values. For little Indians."
- Card 3: Product shot + "Shop the Miko Series →"

### Ad Type 3: Gifting Hook
**Format:** Single image
**Visual:** Beautiful product shot of 5 books wrapped with a ribbon
**Headline:** "The most thoughtful Indian baby gift you can give"
**Copy:** "Miko teaches Indian culture from the very first page. Perfect for baby showers, 1st birthdays, and Diwali gifts."
**CTA:** Shop Now

### Ad Type 4: Social Proof (Use After Reviews)
**Format:** Testimonial screenshot or customer quote as graphic
**Content:** Real review: "My daughter now asks for 'Miko book' every night!"
**Headline:** "₹599 for a lifetime of Indian bedtime stories"

## The WhatsApp Ads Opportunity

Once you have 500+ people in your WhatsApp flow, Meta's **Click-to-WhatsApp ads** become extremely powerful:
- Ad shows in Instagram/Facebook feed
- Click opens a WhatsApp conversation with you directly
- You send the free activity kit
- You have their number permanently

This is the most effective lead gen format for Indian audiences where WhatsApp is the dominant communication channel.

Cost to test: ₹300–500/day. When ready (Phase 2), this should be your first Meta campaign.

---

## Summary: The Decision Rule

> **Run Meta ads when you can answer YES to all 3 of these:**
> 1. Do you have something valuable to give in exchange for contact details? (Lead magnet)
> 2. Does your Amazon listing have 10+ reviews so it converts when people arrive?
> 3. Do you have at least 3 real customer photos or testimonials for ad creative?
>
> If any answer is NO — spend that budget on influencers instead.
> Influencer spend at this stage gives you reviews, UGC, and warm leads. Meta can't do all three.
