# Lead Generation — WhatsApp & Email Strategy

> Goal: Build an owned audience of parents who give you permission to market to them.
> This audience is worth more than any ad account — you own it forever.

---

## Why Owned Channels Matter Most

- Amazon can change its algorithm — your ranking disappears overnight
- Instagram can reduce reach — your content stops being seen
- Meta can ban your ad account — your spend is gone
- **Your email list and WhatsApp list can never be taken away**

Every parent you convert to an email or WhatsApp subscriber is a customer you can reach directly for the next 10 years.

---

## Part A: The Lead Magnet (What You Give in Exchange for Contact)

### The Free Miko Activity Kit

**What it is:** A beautifully designed 8–12 page printable PDF parents can download and do with their toddler. Free. Instant delivery.

**What's inside:**
1. Miko colouring page (Miko at Diwali)
2. Hindi animals matching worksheet (मछली = fish, etc.)
3. Festival dot-to-dot: Diwali diya
4. Action words flashcards (running, jumping, dancing — from the Actions book)
5. Gods & Goddesses simple colouring (Ganesh, Saraswati)
6. "My Manners" checklist toddler poster
7. Hindi alphabet tracing page (basic 5 letters)
8. Read-aloud guide: "How to read with your toddler for 10 minutes a day"

**Cost to create:** 2–3 hours in Canva. Free.

**Perceived value:** High. Parents genuinely use activity kits. They print and do them with their child. This builds brand affinity.

### How to Deliver It
- Website: Email opt-in form → automated email with PDF download link
- WhatsApp: "Message us and we'll send you the free kit" → automated reply with PDF link
- Instagram: "Link in bio" → your website lead capture page

## Part B: WhatsApp Strategy

### Building the List from Zero

**Channel 1: Instagram Bio (Start Today)**
Change bio link to a WhatsApp click-to-chat URL:
`https://wa.me/91XXXXXXXXXX?text=Hi%2C+send+me+the+free+Miko+activity+kit`

When someone taps, WhatsApp opens with a pre-written message. They hit send → you have their number → send them the PDF.

**Channel 2: Website Button**
Add a WhatsApp button to:
- Homepage header: "Get Free Activity Kit on WhatsApp →"
- Every book product page: "Questions? Chat on WhatsApp"
- Thank you page after purchase: "Join the Miko Parents Club on WhatsApp"

**Channel 3: QR Code in Books** (next print run)
Inside front cover, add:
```
"Join the Miko Parents Club — free activities every week!"
[QR code linking to WhatsApp click-to-chat]
```
Every book sold brings you a WhatsApp lead. Books become a lead generation asset.

**Channel 4: Facebook Groups**
When you post in Indian parenting Facebook groups, end with:
"I'm building a small WhatsApp community for parents interested in Indian cultural learning for toddlers — DM me if you'd like to join."

**Channel 5: Every Amazon Buyer**
Package insert card (print at home, ₹2/card):
```
Thank you for welcoming Miko into your home 🙏
Join the Miko Parents Club on WhatsApp
Scan the QR code for weekly activities, Hindi tips,
and early access to new books.
[QR CODE]
— Team NitiVidya
```

### WhatsApp Content Plan (What to Send)

**The Golden Rule:** 3 value messages for every 1 promotional message.

**Month 1: Build Trust**

| Week | Message |
|------|---------|
| Week 1 | Welcome + Free Activity Kit PDF |
| Week 2 | "Hindi tip of the week: 5 words to teach your toddler this week" (image) |
| Week 3 | "Festival this week: [relevant festival] — a 30-second explanation for toddlers" |
| Week 4 | "Parents loved this" — share a customer photo + review (once you have some) |

**Month 2: Soft Introduction to Products**

| Week | Message |
|------|---------|
| Week 5 | Activity sheet #2 (free) |
| Week 6 | "How Miko was born" — short brand story message |
| Week 7 | Read-aloud video clip from a Miko book (30 seconds) |
| Week 8 | "Get the full Miko series — available on Amazon" + link |

**Ongoing: The 4-Week Rotation**
- Week 1: Free resource (activity, printable, guide)
- Week 2: Educational content (culture tip, parenting tip)
- Week 3: Community moment (customer photo, poll)
- Week 4: Product/offer message

### WhatsApp Technical Setup
- Use **WhatsApp Business** (verified) — you have this already
- Set up **Quick Replies** for common questions:
  - "What age are the books for?" → pre-saved response
  - "Do you ship outside India?" → pre-saved response
  - "Where can I buy?" → Amazon link + website link
- Use **Labels** to organize contacts: "Bought Bundle", "Bought Single", "Lead", "Influencer"
- Use **Broadcast Lists** — NOT group chat. Broadcasts feel personal. Group chats feel spammy.

### WhatsApp Business Catalog
Set up your product catalog in WhatsApp Business:
- Add all 6 products (5 individual + bundle) with photos, prices, and buy links
- When someone asks "where can I buy," you can share the catalog directly

## Part C: Email Strategy

### Platform Recommendation
Use **Mailchimp** (free up to 500 contacts) or **Brevo** (formerly Sendinblue, free up to 300/day).

### Email Capture Points on Website
1. **Homepage above fold**: "Download Free Miko Activity Kit — Free for Indian parents"
2. **Exit intent popup**: "Wait — get our free Indian toddler activity kit before you go"
3. **Book product pages**: "Sign up to hear about new Miko books first"
4. **Blog posts**: Inline form at bottom of each post

### Welcome Sequence (Automated — Set Up Once)
Triggered the moment someone subscribes:

**Email 1 (Immediate):** Activity kit delivery
Subject: "Your free Miko activity kit is here 🎨"
Content: PDF download link + "Who is Miko?" 3-sentence intro

**Email 2 (Day 3):** Brand story
Subject: "Why we made Miko (a short story)"
Content: The origin story of NitiVidya — personal, emotional, 200 words

**Email 3 (Day 7):** Value content
Subject: "5 ways to make bedtime reading magical (for Indian homes)"
Content: Genuinely useful tips. No sales pitch.

**Email 4 (Day 14):** Social proof + soft sell
Subject: "Parents are reading this to their toddlers right now"
Content: Customer photo + quote + link to Amazon bundle

**Email 5 (Day 21):** New book preview / offer
Subject: "Meet the full Miko series"
Content: Book-by-book overview, bundle link, 1-time discount if you have one

### Monthly Newsletter: "The Miko Letter"
- Send on the 1st of each month
- 3 sections: Indian cultural tip for the month + parent resource + Miko update
- Never purely promotional — always give something useful
- Open rate goal: 30%+ (Indian parenting audience is highly engaged if content is good)
