# Influencer Playbook

> The most cost-effective way to get your first sales, reviews, and awareness.
> A ₹599 book giveaway to the right influencer can drive ₹5,000–50,000 in sales.

---

## Why Influencers Work for NitiVidya

Children's books are a **trust-based purchase**. Parents don't buy books for their toddlers based on ads — they buy based on:
1. Recommendation from another parent they trust
2. Seeing a real child enjoying it
3. A known educator/parenting account endorsing it

Influencers provide all three. One genuine 30-second Reel of a real child enjoying a Miko book is worth months of ads.

---

## Who to Target

### Tier 1: Micro-Influencers (5K–50K followers)
**These are your most important target.** They have:
- Highly engaged audiences (comment section is real, not bots)
- Affordable (usually accept free product only)
- Authentic voice — their audience trusts them completely
- Time to give your product a genuine review

**Ideal account profiles:**
- Indian mom/parent account sharing parenting tips, book reviews, activities
- "Raising bilingual kids" or "Indian culture for NRI families" accounts
- "Playschool teacher India" — huge credibility for children's products
- Indian lifestyle + parenting crossover accounts

**How to find them:**
1. Search Instagram: #IndianParenting, #DesiMom, #BilingualParenting, #ToddlerBooksIndia
2. Look at who's reviewing competing children's books
3. Search YouTube: "children's books India review" — contact those creators too

### Tier 2: Mid-Tier (50K–200K followers)
- May require paid partnership (₹2,000–₹15,000 per post)
- More reach but less personal engagement
- Good for brand awareness once you have reviews
- Do 2–3 of these in Phase 2

### Tier 3: Celebrity Parents (200K+ followers)
- Skip for now — expensive and less conversion-focused
- Will approach in Phase 3 after product-market fit is clear

### Content Creators (Not Just Influencers)
- YouTube channels that do "read aloud" videos for children
- Indian parenting podcasters
- School teachers with Instagram/Facebook presence
- Indian parenting bloggers with SEO-ranked websites

## The Outreach Message (DM Template)

Keep it short, personal, and clear. Avoid sounding like a PR blast.

**DM Template (Instagram):**

```
Hi [name]! 👋

Love your content about [specific post/topic you actually liked] —
your audience is exactly who I'm hoping to reach.

We're NitiVidya, a small Indian publishing startup.
We just launched "Miko" — a 5-book series of Indian cultural
picture books for toddlers (festivals, Gods, manners, animals).

I'd love to send you the full set (all 5 books, ₹599 value)
completely free — genuinely hoping your little one enjoys them
and you might feel moved to share your honest thoughts with your audience.

No script, no pressure — just hoping Miko finds a home with families
who care about Indian culture 🙏

Would you be open to it?
— [Your name], NitiVidya
```

**Why this works:**
- References their specific content (not a copy-paste blast)
- Explains what you are in one sentence
- States the offer clearly (free books)
- No pressure — "honest thoughts" signals authenticity
- Closes with a simple yes/no question

**Follow up once after 5 days if no reply. If no reply again, move on.**

## What to Ask Them to Post

When they agree, send them a brief (NOT a script):

```
Hi [name]! So excited to send Miko your way 🎉

No script from us — we genuinely want your honest take.

If you do share, a few things that would be helpful:
- @nitividya tag in the post
- Link to Amazon in bio (we'll share the link)
- The #MikoBooks hashtag if you feel like it

We'll share your post on our Instagram too — love supporting each other!

Books dispatched to [address] — should arrive in [days].
```

**What NOT to do:**
- Don't ask for a specific caption
- Don't demand a certain number of posts
- Don't ask them to call it an "ad" unless you're paying them (check Indian advertising standards)
- Don't follow up more than twice

## Where to Send Influencer Traffic (Decision Framework)

Refer to `02-six-questions-answered.md` Q6 for full reasoning.

**Phase 1 (Now — first 3 months):** Send to Amazon
- Ask influencer to put the Amazon link in their bio during the post period
- Reason: drives sales + reviews + Amazon BSR improvement
- UTM: `?tag=nitividya-[influencerhandle]` (if you have Amazon associates)

**Phase 2 (Once 10+ reviews exist):** Split
- Amazon link for direct purchase (primary)
- Website link to free activity kit (secondary, for lead capture)
- "Link in bio: buy on Amazon OR get our free activity kit at nitividya.com"

**Hybrid ask (best approach):**
"If you're doing a story, could you share both links — the Amazon link for parents who want to buy, and our website link for the free activity kit?"

## Tracking Influencer Performance

For each influencer, track:

| Field | What to Track |
|-------|--------------|
| Handle | @username |
| Followers | Number |
| Niche | Parenting / Books / Culture |
| Date contacted | DD/MM/YY |
| Status | Sent DM / Agreed / Books shipped / Posted / No reply |
| Post date | When they posted |
| Estimated reach | Their follower count × avg view rate |
| Amazon clicks | Track via Associates link |
| Reviews received | Did their audience leave reviews? |
| Notes | Anything specific |

Maintain this as a spreadsheet (create `influencer-tracker.xlsx` or use a Google Sheet).

## What Good Results Look Like

| Influencer Size | Expected Views | Expected Clicks | Expected Sales |
|----------------|---------------|-----------------|----------------|
| 5K followers | 500–2,000 | 20–100 | 2–10 |
| 20K followers | 2,000–8,000 | 80–400 | 8–40 |
| 50K followers | 5,000–20,000 | 200–1,000 | 20–100 |
| 100K followers | 10,000–40,000 | 400–2,000 | 40–200 |

*With 0 reviews, conversion is 30–50% lower. With 10+ reviews, conversion is much closer to the top end.*
