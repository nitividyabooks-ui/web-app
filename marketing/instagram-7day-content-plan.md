> **Who you are:** Solo mom entrepreneur with a 2-year-old, building NitiVidya from your room.

> **Goal:** Rebuild organic reach, stop looking like ads, start building community.

---

## CRITICAL FIXES BEFORE YOU START

### Stop Using 4 Accounts to Engage With Your Own Posts
**This is seriously hurting you.** Instagram's algorithm detects:
- Same device / same IP liking, sharing, commenting on the same post
- This is called "engagement pod behavior" and Instagram **penalizes** it
- Your reach is likely being throttled BECAUSE of this, not despite it
- It can also lead to **shadowban** — your content won't show in hashtags or Explore

**Action:** Stop immediately. Use ONLY the @nitividya account from your device. If you want to use personal accounts to share, do it from different devices/IPs and not within the first hour of posting.

### Stop Boosting Reels (For Now)
- Boosted reels train Instagram to only show your content to PAID audiences
- Once you stop paying, reach drops to near zero
- Instagram marks your account as an "advertiser" internally
- **Action:** No more boosting until you have 1,000+ organic followers and consistent 2K+ views

### The Recovery Plan
It takes 2-3 weeks of consistent, non-promotional content to "reset" the algorithm's perception of your account. This 7-day plan is Week 1 of that recovery.

## FILMING TIPS FOR YOUR ROOM SETUP

### Lighting
- Sit facing a window for natural light (best: morning or late afternoon)
- No overhead lights only — they create shadows under your eyes
- If filming at night: one desk lamp pointed at your face from the side

### Background
- Clean but not sterile — a bookshelf, plants, or colorful wall works
- Keep Miko books casually visible in the background (don't center them)
- A cozy corner with cushions for read-aloud videos

### Camera
- Phone on a stack of books or a basic tripod (₹300 on Amazon)
- Eye level or slightly above
- Selfie mode for talking-to-camera, back camera for book/child shots

### Your Child in Videos
- Never force it — film when they're naturally engaged
- Keep a "clip bank" — whenever your child does something cute with a book or during play, hit record
- 3-second clips are gold for B-roll in Reels
